After sessions finish, hit **Run Analysis** from your study dashboard. Humanize processes everything — usually takes a few minutes. You'll get notified when it's ready.

<Info>
  You can run analysis after each session or wait until all sessions are done. Analyzing everything together gives you better cross-session insights.
</Info>

## Qualitative depth, quantitative data

Humanize doesn't just give you quotes and themes. It counts them. Every theme and finding carries how many participants expressed it, so you can tell a pattern from a one-off — the depth of a focus group with the structure you'd expect from a survey.

Counts, not percentages: on a sample of twelve, "58%" implies a precision the sample doesn't have, so findings say "7 of 12" instead.
